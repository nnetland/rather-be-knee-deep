## Brief
- **Problem:** Nicole is repeatedly asked "where should we ski?" (and, per scope-revision-1,
  "where's it dumping this week?") by friends/family and wants a no-auth, link-shareable web app
  across three surfaces: **Plan a trip**, **Chase the snow**, and **Nicole's list**.
- **Primary personas:** newer skiers on a first real trip, families, mixed-ability groups
  (including the person deputised to choose for a group that disagrees), and powder hounds
  including Nicole herself — see Personas below. None of these audiences are secondary; the
  audience genuinely spans all of them per scope-revision-1 §2.
- **Success metrics (Director's priority order, scope-revision-1):** (1) Nicole stops repeating
  herself in DMs; (2) recommendations are genuinely good — accuracy over polish; (3) results get
  shared onward under their own steam; (4) Nicole herself reaches for the snow-chasing mode in a
  real storm cycle — if she doesn't, that mode failed.
- **Brief documents:** `docs/designpowers/briefs/2026-08-03-ski-reco-app.md` (original) and
  `docs/designpowers/briefs/2026-08-03-scope-revision-1.md` (authoritative on conflicts).

## Personas
Formalized via the `inclusive-personas` skill — full detail in
`docs/designpowers/personas/2026-08-03-ski-reco-personas.md`. Five personas, disability and
situational constraint woven into each rather than carved off into a separate "accessible"
persona:

1. **Priya Chandran** — first real trip, non-native English speaker, situational cognitive load
   (planning in stolen minutes between meetings). Primary.
2. **Dana Okafor** — family trip organiser, low vision, situational bright-outdoor-glare use,
   evaluating ski-school claims for a hearing-aid-wearing child. Primary.
3. **Marcus Webb** — deputised to choose for a five-person group that disagrees; permanent
   wrist tendinitis (no sliders, no drag, generous tap targets only). Edge case.
4. **Jordan Reyes** — powder hound, deuteranopia (shape+label difficulty encoding, never
   color-only), checks forecasts with cold/gloved hands and patchy signal. Edge case, and the
   primary Chase-the-Snow persona.
5. **Nicole (Director, as user)** — success criterion #4 made concrete: chasing a storm
   mid-mountain, cold-numbed fingers, deciding whether to book before a window closes; the one
   persona whose trust in the Canada-coverage handling (§5 of strategy.md) is a direct product
   risk if it's wrong.

## Design Principles
Ratified by design-strategist (`docs/designpowers/strategy.md` §6). Design-scout's original three
are ratified as-is; principle 4 is revised (the original "reassure, don't just rank" was correct
only for the pre-revision beginner/family-only audience); principle 5 is new, formalizing a rule
that recurred three separate times in the source material.

1. **The result screen is the product** — both the trip-planner result card and the snow-chase
   list must work as standalone shareable artifacts, screenshotted with zero app chrome.
2. **Grounded data and one trusted human's voice, never either alone** — the numbers give
   credibility, Nicole's Hot Take gives the numbers a reason to be trusted; neither substitutes
   for the other.
3. **Ask about the group, not just the asker** — mixed-ability composition is asked first,
   weighted highest (35/100), and scored with min-max bottleneck logic, never averaged into
   mediocrity (see strategy.md §3.2).
4. **Match the pace and tone to the job, not one voice for both modes** _(revised)_ — Plan-a-Trip
   is unhurried and reassurance-forward; Chase-the-Snow is fast, scannable, and assumes
   competence. No single uniform "brand voice" applied identically to both.
5. **Absence is honest; fabrication is the one unforgivable move** _(new)_ — no invented hot
   takes, no guessed nulls, no historical data dressed up as a live forecast, anywhere in either
   mode.

## Taste Profile
Synthesized by design-lead from the Director's chosen direction (Base Camp Bright,
`design/inspiration-directions.md`) plus the explicit tuning note in scope-revision-1 and
strategy.md — no separate `design-taste` calibration session has run, but the source material was
specific enough (full palette/type/shape spec + an explicit "confident/energetic, never cute"
correction) to build a real taste direction from rather than defaulting to general standards.
**Direction:** Base Camp Bright's rounded-consumer-app DNA, tightened. Keep: rounded geometry,
saturated 4–5-colour accent discipline, colour-tinted shadows, Baloo 2 + Manrope. Cut: ExtraBold
type everywhere (reserved for one use — the result card's resort name), fully pill-shaped
buttons/cards (pills now reserved for tags/badges only), 24–32px squircle radii (tightened to an
8/12/16/20px scale), spring/overshoot motion and confetti (banned system-wide), the blob-person
mascot (dropped — illustrations are place-portraits, no figures). Added: a dedicated monospace
numeral face (IBM Plex Mono, tabular figures) for every stat/data readout — the concrete answer to
"numbers should look like instruments, not decorations" — and genuine hairline borders on cards
(reversing BCB's "shadow only" rule), both serving outdoor/bright-sunlight legibility and a more
engineered feel. Full rationale table in `design/visual-system.md` §0.
